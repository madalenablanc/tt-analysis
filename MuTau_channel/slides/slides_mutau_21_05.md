# Current results — $\mu\tau_h$ channel

- Signal: $pp \to p(\gamma\gamma \to \tau^+\tau^-)p$, $\sqrt{s} = 13$ TeV, 54.9 fb$^{-1}$ (2018)
- BDT-based analysis (Scan A: AdaBoost, $N=600$, $d=2$)
- $\sigma_\text{SM} = 4.7$ fb, per-arm proton selection

| Quantity | Value |
